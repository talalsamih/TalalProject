About This Work

"This book represents the culmination of fifteen years of research at the intersection of computer science, linguistics, philosophy, and theology. The discovery that the Arabic word for God (Allah) shares its etymological root with 'machine' (Alat) opened a door to understanding reality itself as fundamentally computational. What began as linguistic curiosity evolved into a comprehensive theory uniting ancient wisdom with cutting-edge science. My hope is that this work bridges the false divide between faith and reason, showing how both point to the same profound truth: we exist within a vast computational system, and consciousness itself is the universe computing its way into awareness."

— Dr. Talal SAMIH
